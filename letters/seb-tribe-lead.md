I would like to join SEB as a Tribe Lead.

Majority of my professional life I have worked with data and people and in a position at ICOR I see two things that interest me the most combined. Lots of data plus a team to manage. I feel that with the experience and knowledge I have collected I am a suitable candidate for the role, and I would like to give some examples why I think so.

At Barclays I have spent six years as a Vice President of Engineering. In my role I take many responsibilities but mainly I am an Architect, Leader of an Engineering team and occasionally a Product Owner.

As an Architect I have designed various systems with the main goal to collect process and deliver data to the end users. My team was responsible for building and supporting a framework that purposed for various data collections and monitoring across the Bank.

The applications that my team has developed processes ~2.5TB of data daily. The data is collected from various sources, normalized, deduplicated, enriched and delivered to the end users. The data process flow is distributed across a set of nodes and can scale up if there is a need.

As a Product Owner I own various data collection products deployed at the bank. I make sure that the product strategy is aligned with the organization’s goals and that the tactical decisions taken by the engineers fall in line with the product strategy. I design plans for the product development, own the roadmap and am fully responsible for the delivery of the final product.

As a Team Leader for the engineers I make sure that my team is ready to take on new challenges. I manage hiring and onboarding new people, keep track of performance, set quarterly and yearly goals, do salary reviews, and conduct periodical 1 on 1 sessions with the team members to make sure that they are happy and productive.

I hope that you will find my experience interesting and we can meet and discuss the opened opportunity that you have.
