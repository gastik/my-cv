Hi,

I would like to apply for a Software Team Lead position at Dematic.

I have started my career as an engineer and fell in love with coding various solutions. However, with time I have moved to managing engineers and found it to be a rewording experience. For the last seven years I have been leading a cross-functional teams of 18 developers responsible for development and delivery of various IT solutions. I still enjoy solving the problems that require coding, but I mainly make sure that my engineering unit is operating at its best.

I believe that if treated in a right manner the engineers will be happy and productive. I build my team on trust and transparency where all team members are valued with the same level of respect.

As an engineer I have worked with various technologies. My career started with Delphi, and I moved around various technologies mostly staying around the Microsoft stack **.Net**, **MSSQL**, **Python** and many others. I have done a huge share of projects with **various databases**. I prefer to work with backend solutions but did deliver some front-page projects. I do not believe that there is a programming language that suits all the roles and sometimes you need to choose proper tools and mix them to get the best out of technologies.

As a **Team Leader for the engineers**, I make sure that my team is ready to take on new challenges. I managed hiring and onboarding, keep track of performance, set quarterly and yearly goals, do salary reviews, and conduct periodical 1 on 1 sessions with the team. I believe that as a manager my job is to help the team achieve their best by removing the blocks along the way.

In the past I have been responsible for a migration to **DevOps** way of working. I have orchestrated a full-scale migration from monolith structure application into microservice architecture in the cloud and a small-scale migration when the company wanted to be as close to DevOps as possible but for various reasons could not fully embrace DevOps. I have worked on shifting the way that the team works into a more **modern software development** approach. This included shifting from keeping the source locally on developer’s machines into one **centralized source control** repository, manual builds to **automated builds**. Monolith architecture to container based microservice architecture. Manual once per month deployment to continues weekly deployment cycles and eventually to **on demand deployment**.

Most projects were done on the Google cloud platform, but in addition to that I have also used Amazon cloud.

I am in love with **Agile and DevOps** methods and try to live by them. I am certified Scrum Master and strongly believe that Agile can help teams achieve their peak performance and bring control and predictability for the stakeholders. I strongly believe in **Agile values** such as Transparency, Trust, Respect and Embracing the Change.

I hope that my experience makes me a perfect candidate for the role and that we will speak soon. My CV is attached to the letter.
