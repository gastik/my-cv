# Motivation Letter — Engineering Manager, RC Capital

Dear RevenueCat Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Engineering Manager, RC Capital role because it sits directly at the intersection of the areas where I have built most of my career: engineering leadership, financial-services systems, data-intensive products, hands-on technical work, and product delivery in environments where correctness and risk matter.

I spent more than six years at Barclays as Vice President of Engineering and Head of Asset Inventory and Discovery. I led a cross-location engineering organization of 18 people, owned product strategy and delivery, hired and developed engineers, worked with stakeholders across the bank, and remained involved in architecture and technical decisions. The systems my team built collected, normalized, deduplicated, enriched, and distributed large volumes of enterprise data, with approximately 2.5 TB processed each day. I also supported regulatory reporting and designed internal audit processes, so I am accustomed to engineering decisions being evaluated not only for speed and functionality but also for traceability, reliability, and risk.

One of the projects that best reflects how I work was a regulatory software-inventory program I inherited after it had already been running for two years. It was overdue, over budget, the original team had left, and the existing infrastructure could not handle the required load. I rebuilt the scope with stakeholders, formed the delivery team, drove a sequence of technical prototypes, secured additional funding when necessary, and delivered the system in time for the external audit. That experience shaped how I approach ambiguous, high-pressure work: reduce the problem to what matters, make technical risks visible early, create clear ownership, and keep shipping toward a measurable outcome.

My earlier engineering background is also directly relevant to RC Capital. At Sermo I worked on integrations with external payment systems, including Amazon and Travelex-related payment flows, and introduced fraud-detection and prevention processes. SQL and database engineering have remained core technical strengths throughout my career. More recently I returned to hands-on Senior Data Engineering work, using modern AI-assisted engineering tools alongside conventional development and review practices. I am comfortable moving between people leadership, product prioritization, code and data investigation, and detailed technical discussions with engineers.

RevenueCat's decision to build RC Capital as a zero-to-one financial-services business is particularly interesting to me. Early Payouts and the future work around risk monitoring, money movement, reconciliation, banking integrations, and fraud require an engineering organization that can move quickly without treating financial correctness and operational controls as secondary concerns. That combination of speed, technical depth, customer focus, and risk-sensitive execution is where I believe my background is strongest.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience leading distributed engineering teams, building data-intensive systems in a regulated bank, working with payments and fraud, and staying technically hands-on could contribute to RC Capital.

Sincerely,  
Igor Popov
