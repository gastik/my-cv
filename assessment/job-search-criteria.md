# Job assessment criteria

Use these criteria when screening or ranking opportunities for this CV project.

- Prefer **fully remote** roles.
- Company country is not a restriction if the employer can credibly hire or contract a Lithuania-based candidate.
- Compensation floor: **€100,000 annual total cash/base-equivalent**.
- Priority role families:
  - Engineering Manager
  - Data Engineering Manager
  - Head of Data / Head of Data Platform
  - Technical product leadership
  - AI Product Manager
  - Fintech / payments / data-platform leadership
- YC-company roles are in scope.

## Assessment output

For every job ad, separate:
1. hard eligibility constraints,
2. evidence-backed fit,
3. gaps or unverified requirements,
4. compensation/remote-location confidence,
5. recommended CV source material and achievements to emphasize.
